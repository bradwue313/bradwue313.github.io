Questions

Short answers to what people usually ask first.

What if my problem doesn't fit a package?

Email aurelis.work@gmail.com or message me on GitHub before paying and describe it. If it's out of scope or I can't help, I'll say so up front.

Which languages and stacks?

Mostly Rust, Solana and Anchor, Go, and TypeScript. Smart contract work is my strongest area.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Tell me what's wrong and I'll revise it. If the fix doesn't hold up, email aurelis.work@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.

Is the security review an audit?

No. It's an independent review by one engineer. It's useful for catching real bugs, but it isn't a substitute for a formal audit before launching something that holds significant funds.

Terms in brief

Plain language, not legal advice.

Scope and delivery

Each package covers the work described for it. If your task turns out to be larger than the package, I'll tell you before going further and you can decide whether to upgrade. Work is delivered as a pull request, a patch, or a written report, depending on the package.

Access and confidentiality

I'll only use repository access to do the agreed work, and I won't share your code or findings with anyone else. For public projects, I'll disclose any security issue only in the way you agree to.

No guarantees about security

A review reduces risk but can't prove code is free of bugs. Reviews and fixes are provided as they are, without warranty, and I'm not liable for losses arising from how the code is later used.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe and charged when you check out. Monthly retainers renew until you cancel.

Refunds and revisions

If a delivered fix fails to solve the agreed problem, I will revise it at no extra charge. If a bug proves to be outside our diagnostic capabilities or cannot be resolved within the scope, you will receive a 100% refund. Monthly retainers can be cancelled anytime with zero hassle.
